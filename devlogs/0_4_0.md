# Oblada 0.4.0: devlog

Not released yet. Based on Chromium 156.0.8078.48. What changed since 0.3.2, in detail; the
public release notes are in [`changelogs/0_4_0.json`](../changelogs/0_4_0.json).

## Expanded scaffold, a fourth window layout

- `oblada::sidebar::Mode::kExpandedScaffold` (`oblada/browser/ui/sidebar_mode.{h,cc}`,
  `"expanded_scaffold"` in `oblada.sidebar.mode`): the toolbar a row at the top of the
  window, next to the window controls, as in scaffold mode, and the vertical tab strip
  expanded below it, the full sidebar but for its toolbar: the tabs with their titles, the
  workspaces in a row, the media playing as cards.
- `IsCollapsed()` says which layouts collapse the strip (scaffold, compact), `IsScaffold()`
  which have the toolbar at the top (scaffold, expanded scaffold).
- The browser layout (`browser_view_tabbed_layout_impl.cc`) lays it out as scaffold:
  `TransientLayoutData::oblada_scaffold` is now either scaffold, and `oblada_sidebar` is
  false for an expanded strip in expanded scaffold mode. The strip is as wide as Chromium
  says, the sidebar's width; only collapsed scaffold sets its contents narrow
  (`SetExpansion()`). The region view (`vertical_tab_strip_region_view.cc`,
  `IsObladaSidebar()`) leaves out its row of buttons in both.
- The strip is against the window's edge and the page, as the sidebar is, rather than
  within scaffold's margins: its resize area, along its right edge, is then at the page's
  edge, where the sidebar's is. Scaffold's margins come in as it collapses, from how far
  its width is from the sidebar's to the narrow one (`oblada_scaffold_margin`), so that ⌘⇧L
  moves the tabs smoothly from one to the other.
- The resize area wasn't there at first: Chromium puts it at `bounds().right()`, the
  strip's right edge in its parent, which is its width only when the strip starts at the
  window's edge. In scaffold's margins it was 8 px past the strip, out of reach, and the
  sidebar couldn't be resized. It's now at the strip's own right edge (`width()`).
- Collapsing and expanding the strip (⌘⇧L, the sidebar's menu; `ModeKeeper`): expanded
  scaffold collapses into scaffold, the address bar staying at the top; the sidebar, as
  before, into the collapsed layout last chosen (`oblada.sidebar.collapsed_mode`).
  Expanding goes to the expanded layout last chosen, a new pref
  (`oblada.sidebar.expanded_mode`), the sidebar by default: scaffold and compact expanded
  into the sidebar until now.
- Settings > Oblada and the menu's "Window layout ▸" list it after scaffold ("Expanded
  scaffold", "Scaffold espanso", `l10n.cc`), and the welcome shows it at work in a
  little window of its own (`.m-le` in `welcome_page.cc`): the address bar on top, another tab picked
  in the sidebar below it.
- `screenshot.mjs --mode=expanded_scaffold`, and the action `mode:expanded_scaffold`. A new
  action, `resize:<dx>` (`window_snapshots.cc`, `Resize()`), drags the sidebar's edge, as
  the pointer would, through the window's hit testing.
- The smoke test checks the layout, that dragging the sidebar's edge resizes it, ⌘⇧L from
  it to scaffold and back, and picks it in the welcome rather than scaffold.

## Horizontal tabs: horizon, horizon compact and classic

Three more layouts (`oblada::sidebar::Mode::kHorizon`, `kHorizonCompact`, `kClassic`,
`"horizon"`, `"horizon_compact"`, `"classic"` in `oblada.sidebar.mode`) with Chromium's
horizontal tab strip, made as plain as the sidebar (`oblada/browser/ui/horizontal_tabs.{h,cc}`):

- **Horizon**, as Safari's: the toolbar in a row at the top, next to the window controls, its
  address bar in the middle of the window, narrower (`CenterLocationBar()`: 46% of the
  window, 420 to 760 wide); the tabs share the row below it evenly, however wide
  (`kHorizonTabMaxWidth`), their icon and title in the middle.
- **Horizon compact**, as Safari's compact layout: the tabs in the toolbar's row, where its
  layout would put the address bar (`CompactTabsRoom()`, from the toolbar's
  `FlexLayout::GetProposedLayout()`), sharing that room as horizon's share their row
  (`SharesRow()`), the address bar over the active one, short of its close button
  (`LayOutCompactLocationBar()`), following it as tabs move (`ActiveTabWatcher`). The bar is
  the sidebar's kind (`HasSidebarLocationBar()`): quiet (the site only, the container left to
  the tab's mark) until hovered, and floating over the page while it has the focus, its shape
  flying from the tab and back (the floating bar's place is now given,
  `LayOutLocationBar(toolbar, bar, slot)`). It has no background of its own over the tab's
  veil, and the tab shows nothing of its own under it but its close button
  (`Tab::IsObladaUnderLocationBar()`). The tab strip's region comes before the toolbar for
  events, which went to the tab under the bar: a click didn't focus the bar, nor did hovering
  unquiet it (and show the site panel's button). `CompactTabsTargeter`, the region's, leaves
  them to the bar. While it floats, the toolbar's container goes above the page but
  stays below the tabs (`SetToolbarAbovePage()`'s `tabs`): topmost, as in the compact
  sidebar, it took the clicks of the whole row, the tabs' among them. Crowded, the other
  tabs shrink to their icons, the active one no narrower
  than `kCompactActiveTabMinWidth` (140) while the others fit (`CompactActiveTabMinWidth()`),
  which every tab tells, for them to stay as wide as each other with room to spare: Chromium
  only repaints tabs when another one becomes active then (`TabContainerImpl::SetActiveTab()`),
  so a wider active tab only widened at the next hover. No hover cards while the bar floats,
  its suggestions under it.
- **Classic**: the tabs in a row at the top, next to the window controls, the toolbar below
  them.

The rows take 80 points in horizon and classic modes (Chrome's take 86), 46 in horizon
compact mode: the tabs' veils are as far from the address bar as it is from the page
(`kRowGap`), the toolbar's margins and the tab strip's padding overlapping. The page floats
below them with the margins and corners it has next to the sidebar.

- `sidebar_mode.{h,cc}`: the new modes, `IsHorizontal()`, `HasHorizontalTabs()`; the
  horizontal layouts aren't remembered as expanded or collapsed ones, and `ModeKeeper` leaves
  the vertical strip alone meanwhile. The menu's "Window layout ▸" puts them after a
  separator, Settings > Oblada under "Tabs at the top".
- `vertical_tab_strip_state_controller_impl.cc`: vertical tabs follow the layout's pref
  rather than Chromium's (`SetVerticalTabsEnabled()` does nothing), and the strip is only
  switched when the layout goes from vertical to horizontal tabs or back: switching between
  two vertical layouts reset a tab strip that wasn't there and crashed.
- `browser_view_tabbed_layout_impl.cc`: `TransientLayoutData::oblada_horizontal`, the rows,
  and the tab strip's region and the toolbar marked with the layout
  (`oblada::horizontal::SetLayout()`, read back with `LayoutOf()` by the tabs, the toolbar and
  the hover cards). The toolbar is see-through over the glass, as in the sidebar.
- `browser_native_widget_mac.mm`: the glass covers the whole window with horizontal tabs too,
  as lightly tinted as with the sidebar (Chromium gave it the top of the window only, an
  opaque view below), so the workspace's theme shows all around.
- The tabs (`tab.cc`, `horizontal_tab_style_views.cc`): veils of `tab_style.cc`'s colors, the
  active one rounded as the others rather than flowing into the toolbar, without outlines or
  lines between them; the close button on the active tab and the one under the pointer only;
  the container's color as a thin bar at the bottom of the veil
  (`oblada::containers::PaintTabIndicator()`). Hover cards stay, as titles are often cut
  short (`tab_hover_card_controller.cc`).
- `horizontal_tab_strip_region_view.{h,cc}`: no tab search button nor the button leaving the
  focused group; no room kept at the end of the tabs to grab the window by in horizon mode.
  The pill is in the region's children in z-order (`GetChildrenInZOrder()`), which is what
  gets painted and gets events: without it, a click on the pill went to the region, which
  the smoke test missed by clicking the pill directly (it now clicks where the window would,
  `press:`).
- The new tab button of horizontal tabs (`tabs/new_tab_button.{h,cc}`, not the sidebar's
  `tabs/shared/new_tab_button.cc`) shows the containers when held down, as the sidebar's
  does (`ContainersMenuModel::ForNewTab()`). `window_snapshots.cc` has `hold:<x>,<y>` for
  the smoke test to check it.
  `tab_container_impl.cc` and `tab_group_header.cc` hide the workspaces' group headers.
- In horizon modes, closing a tab with the pointer doesn't freeze the others' widths
  (`TabContainerImpl::EnterTabClosingMode()`, Chromium's "tab closing mode", which keeps the
  next close button under the pointer): it left room at the end of the row until the pointer
  was well away, 40 points below the tabs, which is the page there.
- **The workspace's pill** (`oblada/browser/workspaces/workspace_pill.{h,cc}`,
  `WorkspacePill`), where the tab search button was: the workspace's icon and name on a veil
  and a chevron (its icon only in horizon compact mode). A click lists the workspaces and
  "New workspace"; a right click opens the workspace's menu, as its button in the sidebar
  does; a two-finger swipe across the tabs shows the next or previous workspace (60 points).
- Without the sidebar: Chromium's media button in the toolbar shows with horizontal tabs only
  (`toolbar_view.cc`), and its downloads button too (`downloads::SidebarButton()` is null
  then, `downloads_button.cc`).
- `window_snapshots.cc`: swipes go to the horizontal tabs where there's no sidebar.
- The smoke test checks each layout's rows, the centered address bar, the address bar over
  the active tab and floating once focused, the pill's list, swipes across the tabs, and
  going back to the sidebar.

## The welcome's layouts

Seven layouts didn't fit side by side: the layout step (`welcome_page.cc`) now lists them by
where the tabs are ("Tabs on the side", "Tabs at the top"), and shows the one picked, or the
one under the pointer, at work in a little window next to the list (`.previews`). The
horizontal layouts have little windows of their own: another tab picked in horizon and
classic modes, and in horizon compact mode the address bar sliding to it.

## Fixes

- **Extension popups show from the site panel's button in every layout**
  (`site_panel.cc`, `ExtensionsAnchor()`): only in the sidebar until now, elsewhere from the
  toolbar's extensions container, kept out of sight at the top left of the window.
- **The welcome and the New Tab page stay see-through when reloaded**
  (`contents_web_view.cc`). Reloading one gives it a new document with a view of its own,
  which takes its see-through color from the last one (`TakeFallbackContentFrom()`), but the
  renderer is told only as a view's color changes, and only through its page's widget, the
  old one then: it painted its default background, dark. The view of a see-through page is
  now made opaque, then see-through again, whenever its background is set.
