# Oblada 0.1.3: devlog

Not released yet. Based on Chromium 155.0.8059.85. What changed since 0.1.2, in detail; the
public release notes are in [`changelogs/0_1_3.json`](../changelogs/0_1_3.json).

## Internal pages

- `oblada://version` is Oblada's at the top too: its logo with its name (the payment dialogs'
  wordmark, 32 points high, a white one for the dark theme), drawn by `branding/export.sh`
  into `chromium/overrides/components/resources/default_{100,200}_percent/chromium/product_logo{,_white}.png`,
  over Chromium's; and "The Oblada Authors" with Oblada's copyright, the About page's
  (`oblada::about::Authors()`, `Copyright()`, l10n's `about.authors`; `version_ui.cc`).
  Chromium stays credited in the version line, `oblada://credits` and the About page.
- No "(Developer Build)" after the version (`about_version.html`): Chromium says so of any
  build that isn't Google's official one, Oblada's releases included.
- The smoke test checks the logo (141 points wide, not Chromium's 171), the build label and
  that "Chromium Authors" is gone from the page.
