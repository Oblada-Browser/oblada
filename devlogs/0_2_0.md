# Oblada 0.2.0: devlog

Not released yet. Based on Chromium 156.0.8078.48. What changed since 0.1.2, in detail; the
public release notes are in [`changelogs/0_2_0.json`](../changelogs/0_2_0.json).

## Chromium 156

- Oblada moves from Chromium 155.0.8059.85 to 156.0.8078.48 (`chromium/CHROMIUM_VERSION`),
  stable since 2026-10-20, with 353 security fixes (6 critical, 61 high). 155.0.8059.86,
  out the same day, only changed the version number.
- **Dependencies one commit deep.** `gclient sync --no-history` clones a new dependency
  with its last commit only, but brings an existing one up to date with a plain
  `git fetch`: every branch, with whole histories (over 15 GB, and the disk ran out).
  `chromium/scripts/deps.py` now runs around it in `sync.sh`: `fetch` fetches, one commit
  deep, the commit each dependency needs and checks it out (again for those nested in
  others, whose DEPS change with them), so gclient only checks out; `prune` then drops
  the remote branches, tags and older commits a dependency was given.
- **Patches.** 117 of the 130 applied as they were. The others, by hand:
  - `VerticalTabStripStateController` is an interface in 156, implemented by
    `VerticalTabStripStateControllerImpl`: always-vertical tabs and the minimum width moved
    to `vertical_tab_strip_state_controller_impl.cc` (a new patch, in place of the `.cc`
    one); the "shown expanded" state stays in the interface.
  - The sidebar's `VerticalTabStripRegionView` lays its children out itself
    (`CalculateProposedLayout()`), with the tabs in a `content_area_view_`: the workspace
    switcher goes there, and the row of buttons above the tabs is left out of the layout
    in Oblada's sidebar (`IsObladaSidebar()`) rather than hidden. `WorkspaceSlider` now
    works on the content area (the switcher's parent), which has its own layer.
  - `TabGroupHeaderView` updates its colors in `UpdateColors()`: a workspace's header
    (no chip, the tabs' text color, its icon) is set there; the font where the title is.
  - `//chrome/browser/ui:ui` has its sources in its desktop branch only: Oblada's are
    added there.
  - The tab menu's group items (with 156's focus on ungrouped tabs), the app menu's tab
    orientation item, the AI settings page (now with GEIC), the suggestions' width (now
    also for the full WebUI omnibox frame), the compact sidebar's opacity, the previous
    workspace command and the closed tabs' containers: the same edits on 156's code.
- **Oblada's code.** `TabStripModel::SetFocusedGroup()` is gone: workspaces are shown with
  `EnterFocusMode()` (`workspaces.cc`).

## Media controls

- Media playing in the window's other tabs is controlled at the bottom of the sidebar,
  above the workspaces, as in Zen (`oblada::media::MediaControls`,
  `oblada/browser/ui/media_controls.{h,cc}`), in place of Chromium's media button in the
  toolbar, which is no longer created (`toolbar_view.cc`).
- A card for each tab whose media session is controllable and has played (paused, it
  stays), but the tab shown: its card waits 500 ms after the tab stops being shown, so
  that going through the tabs doesn't make it flicker. None in picture in picture, which
  has its own controls. Each tab's session is followed through `content::MediaSession`
  (`Session`, a `MediaSessionObserver`), bound once the tab plays something.
- Stacked, a card is its row of buttons: previous track, play or pause, next track (dimmed
  if the page has none) and sound. Sound mutes the player (the media session's
  `SetMute()`) when the page has just one (`audio_video_states`): Chromium takes a muted
  tab's media out of its session, so muting the tab would take the card away too. With
  more players it mutes the tab (`SetTabAudioMuted()`, as Zen does); the card then stays
  while the tab is muted, its other buttons dimmed, for the sound to be turned back on.
  The newest card is in front; up to two older ones peek out 8 points above it, each 4%
  smaller and 30% fainter.
- Under the pointer, the cards spread out one under the other (300 ms) and each shows what
  it plays: title, artist (else the site), the time played and the duration either side
  of a bar that can be clicked or dragged to seek (if the page allows it), picture in
  picture (for videos) and a close button, which pauses it and puts the card away until
  it plays again. Clicking a card shows its tab, and with it its workspace.
- Cards come and go animated (`presence()`); the cards' height follows, so the tabs above
  make room. The cards' colors come from the sidebar's: darker in a dark theme, lighter in
  a light one. Strings in l10n's `media.*`. The compact sidebar has none.
- `vertical_tab_strip_region_view.{h,cc}` adds the cards to the content area, above the
  workspace switcher (`oblada_media_`).
- `screenshot.mjs --media=<n>` opens tabs playing silent music with titles and artists
  (`silentWav()` in `lib/oblada.mjs`); the smoke test checks that a background tab's
  music gets a card, that its buttons pause and mute it (the card staying), that clicking
  it shows its tab, and that closing it pauses it until it plays again.

## Internal pages

- `oblada://version` is Oblada's at the top too: its logo with its name (the payment dialogs'
  wordmark, 32 points high, a white one for the dark theme), drawn by `branding/export.sh`
  into `chromium/overrides/components/resources/default_{100,200}_percent/chromium/product_logo{,_white}.png`,
  over Chromium's; and "The Oblada Authors" with Oblada's copyright, the About page's
  (`oblada::about::Authors()`, `Copyright()`, l10n's `about.authors`; `version_ui.cc`).
  Chromium stays credited in the version line, `oblada://credits` and the About page.
- No "(Developer Build)" after the version (`about_version.html`): Chromium says so of any
  build that isn't Google's official one, Oblada's releases included.
- The smoke test checks the logo (141 points wide, not Chromium's 171), the build label and
  that "Chromium Authors" is gone from the page.
