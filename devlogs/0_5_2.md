# Oblada 0.5.2: devlog

Not released yet. Based on Chromium 156.0.8078.48. What changed since 0.5.1, in detail; the
public release notes are in [`changelogs/0_5_2.json`](../changelogs/0_5_2.json).

## Development

- `make free-space` (`chromium/scripts/free_space.sh`, `DRY=1` lists first): removes what
  development leaves outside the builds. Test runs' Oblada left running once their test
  was stopped (the test copy's main processes whose parent is launchd, found by their
  executable), their profiles and folders in the temporary folder (`oblada-*`, and
  add-ons unpacked there over an hour ago), the test apps' settings in `~/Library`,
  Oblada's code-sign clones in `/var/folders/…/X` no running process uses, and
  `/tmp/oblada*`. Never the builds' directories, the website's, the user's profile or its
  caches, nor releases' symbols; it refuses while a test, a build or a release runs.
